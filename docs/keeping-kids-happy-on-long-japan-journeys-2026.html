Keeping Kids Happy on Long Japan Journeys (2026)

To keep kids happy on long journeys through Japan, parents can engage them with a variety of activities and practical preparations tailored for flights and train rides. Here are some tips to ensure boredom doesn't become the enemy on your travels!

  • Pack a selection of kid-friendly snacks and meals.
  • Bring along interactive travel games that can entertain for hours.
  • Utilize train amenities like multi-purpose rooms for families.
  • Look into child fare structures for cost-effective travel.
  • Ensure stroller access and storage on trains is planned in advance.

Engaging Activities for Flights

Long flights can be challenging, especially for children. Here are some engaging activities that will keep them entertained:

1. Travel Games

Portable travel games are a fantastic way to keep kids occupied during flights. Consider games like compact board games, travel bingo, or simple card games. These can often be enjoyed by a small group, allowing even your toddler to join in!

2. Activity Books

Puzzle or coloring books that feature a Japanese theme can spark interest. Look for titles that highlight Japanese culture, animals, or language activities, which can also make the journey educational and fun.

3. Snacks as Currency

Snacks often hold magical powers over children on long flights. Pack a variety of treats—such as rice crackers, fruit gummies, or bento-style meals. Use these snacks as rewards for good behavior or as distractions during turbulence.

4. Download Family Movies

Before your flight, download a couple of family-friendly movies from popular platforms. A good animated film can keep your children entertained for a significant period!

On the Trains (Shinkansen & JR Lines)

Traveling by train in Japan can be a delightful experience for families. Here are some specific tips to help make your journey smoother:

Stroller Access & Storage

Strollers can generally be brought onto Shinkansen trains without an extra fee. For safety:

  • Folded strollers should be stored on overhead racks or at your feet.
  • Unfolded strollers cannot be left in the aisles.

If your stroller is oversized (between 160 cm and 250 cm), you’ll need to reserve a special seat to accommodate it. Starting July 1, 2025, bringing oversized luggage without a reservation incurs a 1,000 yen fee.

Multi-Purpose Rooms (Tamokuteki-shitsu)

Many Shinkansen trains offer multi-purpose rooms for families:

  • These rooms can be used for changing diapers or nursing.
  • Ask the train conductor to unlock the room if it isn't occupied.

While these rooms are prioritized for passengers with disabilities, families can use them if available.

In-Car Amenities

Many train lines offer in-car sales, which often include kid-friendly options. Always check for allergy information before purchasing. Snacks on trains can include items like bento boxes, rice balls, and seasonal fruit.

Keeping Kids Entertained

During the journey, keeping children engaged is key. Consider these options:

1. Download Educational Apps

Apps that teach Japanese language basics or introduce them to Japanese culture can be both fun and educational!

2. Stories about Japan

Books or audiobooks about Japan can spark curiosity. Seek out stories featuring Japanese folklore or adventures in Japan.

3. Themed Travel Journals

Encourage them to keep a travel journal. They can draw pictures of what they see or document their favorite experiences. This not only keeps them occupied but also creates lasting memories of the trip.

FAQ

What child fares apply when traveling in Japan?

Child fares are typically half the adult fare for kids aged 6-11, while toddlers under 5 travel free if they do not occupy a seat.

Can I bring a stroller on the Shinkansen?

Yes, strollers can be brought onto Shinkansen trains without extra fees, but they must be folded and stored properly.

Are there facilities for changing diapers on trains?

Yes, multi-purpose rooms available on most Shinkansen trains can be used for changing diapers or nursing.

What snacks should I bring for the flight?

Consider a mix of healthy snacks and treats, like rice crackers, fruit gummies, and bento-style meals.

Can I reserve seats for oversized luggage?

Yes, it's recommended to reserve a “seat with oversized luggage space” if your stroller exceeds the standard size limits.

Are there any family-friendly activities on the train?

Travel games, activity books, and themed journals are excellent ways to keep kids engaged during the journey.

Final Thoughts

With a little preparation and the right activities, long journeys through Japan can be an enjoyable experience for both kids and parents. Plan ahead, pack wisely, and enjoy your travels!
