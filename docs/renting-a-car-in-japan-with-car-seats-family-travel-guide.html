Renting a Car in Japan with Kids in 2026

If you're traveling with children in Japan, renting a car with appropriate car seats can be essential for your family's safety and comfort. Picture your little one napping peacefully in a cozy car seat, while you enjoy the stunning views of Mount Fuji without the stress of public transport.

  • Car seats are mandatory for children under 6 years old.
  • Rental services often provide car seats for an additional fee.
  • Japan’s road rules may differ from your home country.
  • Consider your itinerary and destinations.
  • Car rentals are available at major airports and cities.

Understanding Japanese Car Seat Regulations

In Japan, children under 6 years old must be secured in an appropriate child restraint system. This means that if you're traveling with toddlers or young children, you need to make arrangements for a car seat. Rental companies typically offer options for various age groups, including rear-facing and booster seats. The cost of renting a car seat can range from ¥1,000 to ¥3,000 per day (as of 2026, confirm on the official site), depending on the type and rental company.

Benefits of Renting a Car with Kids

Renting a car can offer your family flexibility and convenience, especially when visiting remote sites like the serene Shirakawa-go village or the picturesque Nara Park, where deer roam freely. With a car, you can pack up snacks, toys, and all the essentials without worrying about carrying them on public transport. Imagine the ease of loading your toddler's stroller and all the gear right into your vehicle. Plus, the ability to stop whenever you need for a quick snack—trust us, snacks are the currency of family travel!

Choosing the Right Rental Company

When selecting a rental company, consider the following reputable options: Nippon Rent-A-Car, Toyota Rent a Car, or Times Car Rental. They all offer various vehicle sizes and child seat options. Make sure to book in advance, especially during peak travel seasons, to ensure availability of all necessary equipment. You can also rent a stroller, car seat & baby gear in Japan for added convenience.

How to Ensure Safety While Driving

Japan drives on the left side of the road, which may be an adjustment for families from right-hand driving countries. Familiarize yourself with local traffic rules, and always adhere to speed limits. In urban areas, these can be as low as 40 km/h, while highways may allow up to 100 km/h. Your rental car will come equipped with a GPS, but downloading offline maps can be helpful, especially when navigating to more rural areas.

Finding Kid-Friendly Stops Along Your Route

Planning your driving route can be fun! Make sure to include kid-friendly stops. For example, if you’re driving from Tokyo to Hakone, consider a stop at the Fuji-Q Highland amusement park, where you can find thrilling rides and family attractions. Another option is to visit the Fuji Five Lakes area, perfect for picnics and taking in breathtaking views of Mount Fuji. Kids can run around while you enjoy the scenery—just remember to pack some snacks to avoid the classic “Are we there yet?” question!

Using Public Transportation Instead?

While renting a car is advantageous, Japan's public transportation system is world-renowned for its efficiency, punctuality, and ease of use. Trains and buses are equipped with space for strollers, and larger stations have baby-changing facilities. If your itinerary focuses on major cities like Tokyo, Osaka, or Kyoto, public transport may be a more convenient option. However, if you're setting your sights on the countryside or specific attractions, a car will give you the freedom to explore at your leisure.

FAQ

Do I need to rent a car seat in Japan?

Yes, if you’re traveling with children under 6 years old, you’ll need to provide a car seat. Rental companies can supply one.

What are the costs associated with renting a car seat?

Rental car seats typically cost between ¥1,000 and ¥3,000 per day (as of 2026, confirm on the official site).

Can I drive with my child in my lap?

No, it is illegal in Japan for children to ride in a car without a proper child restraint.

Is it easy to find parking in Japan?

Parking varies by location; urban areas may have limited street parking, but there are many paid parking lots.

What if my child has special needs regarding car seats?

Inform the rental company in advance, as they may have specific options available.

Are there tolls on Japanese highways?

Yes, expect tolls on most highways, and prepare to pay with cash or an electronic toll collection card.

Is driving in Japan safe?

Yes, Japan has one of the lowest traffic accident rates in the world, but always follow local traffic laws.

Can I use my own car seat in a rental car?

Yes, you can bring your own car seat, but ensure it meets local safety standards.

In summary, renting a car with a car seat in Japan can enhance your family's travel experience, allowing for flexibility, convenience, and peace of mind on the road. Remember to plan ahead and enjoy the beautiful sights that Japan has to offer!
