Understanding Car Seat Regulations in Japan

In Japan, the law is clear: all children under 6 must be secured in a child seat while traveling. If you fail to comply, you risk a penalty point on your driver's license. Although this may not seem severe, safety should always be the priority. Children aged 6 and older should use booster seats until they reach a height of at least 135 cm, and ideally 140 cm, as adult seatbelts may not provide adequate protection for smaller kids.

The responsibility for ensuring the car seat is installed correctly falls on the driver. While rental staff can assist, it’s critical to perform a final safety check. Familiarizing yourself with the car seat before hitting the road is wise, especially if you're dealing with a tired child who prefers to be anywhere but in a car seat!

Practical Tips for Renting with Kids

1. Book Early: Child seats can go fast, especially during peak travel seasons. Make sure to reserve yours when you book your car to avoid any last-minute surprises.

2. Check Installation: While rental staff can help, it's crucial that you verify the seat is properly installed. Familiarize yourself with the car seat's manual if needed, and don’t hesitate to ask for assistance.

3. Consider Your Itinerary: If your travel plans include a lot of car time, look for routes that have frequent stops for stretching legs and bathroom breaks. Japan’s rest areas often have playgrounds and food options that can make a pit stop more enjoyable.

4. Pack Snacks: Always have your go-to snacks handy. They can make all the difference when the “are we there yet?” chorus starts to kick in!

FAQ

Do I need to bring my own car seat to Japan?

While you can bring your own, it’s often easier to rent a car seat from the rental agency, especially since Japanese law requires proper car seats for kids under 6.

What happens if I don't use a car seat for my child?

Not using a car seat for a child under 6 can lead to a penalty point on your driver's license, though no monetary fine is issued.

Can I rent a car with a car seat at the airport?

Yes, most major airports in Japan have car rental services that offer child seats. It’s advisable to reserve them in advance.

Are there any exceptions to the car seat law?

Exemptions exist for public transport or in emergencies, but generally, it’s safest to adhere to the law and use a car seat.

How much do car seats cost to rent in Japan?

Rental prices typically range from ¥550 to ¥1,650 depending on the type of seat (as of 2026, confirm on the official site).

What if my child is over 6 but still needs a booster seat?

It’s strongly recommended that children continue using a booster seat until they are at least 135 cm tall for their safety.

Can I install the car seat in the front passenger seat?

It’s generally not recommended to install car seats in the front passenger seat due to safety concerns.

What should I do if I have trouble installing the car seat?

If you encounter difficulties, don’t hesitate to ask rental staff for assistance, but remember that the final safety check is your responsibility.

In summary, renting a car with the appropriate child seat in Japan is not just a legal requirement but an important step for keeping your little ones safe on the road. Remember to book your car seats in advance and take the time to ensure they are properly installed—doing so will give you peace of mind as you explore the beautiful sights of Japan with your family.
