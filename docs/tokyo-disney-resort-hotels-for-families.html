Tokyo Disney Resort Hotels for Families (2026)

Every hotel affiliated with Tokyo Disney Resort — the six Disney Hotels, the six Official Hotels inside the resort and the four Partner Hotels in Shin-Urayasu — compared on the one thing that decides a family booking: how many people actually fit in the largest room, how big it is, and how you get to the gate. Room names, floor areas and occupancy below come from each hotel’s own website.

How this list was made. We read the room pages of all sixteen hotels on their official sites in September 2026 and list only what is stated there: room type names, floor area, maximum occupancy and bed configuration, plus the shuttle facts from Tokyo Disney Resort’s own hotel pages. Where a hotel does not publish a room size — Hilton Tokyo Bay, Sheraton Grande and Oriental Hotel Tokyo Bay are the three that do not — we say so rather than copy a number from a booking site. Prices are deliberately not listed; the “See rates” links open a live search. Disney Hotels are booked on the resort’s own site, so they have no rates link here.

Quick picks by family size

  • Six in one room, on Disney property: Disney Ambassador Hotel Family Room (97 m², 6) — the only Disney Hotel room that takes six.
  • Six on a budget, one shuttle stop away: Hotel Emion Tokyo Bay Family6 (67 m², 6) or Urayasu Brighton Hotel Tokyo Bay At Home Room (52 m², 6, with a bunk bed).
  • Bunk beds children will remember: Grand Nikko Nikko Bunk Bed Room (64 m², up to 6) and Sheraton Grande Treasures 4–6 Beds.
  • Two connected rooms as one booking: MAIHAMA VIEW HOTEL Connecting Harmony Room (64 m², 4–6) — the only named connecting type at the resort.
  • Closest to the gate: Tokyo DisneySea Hotel MiraCosta is inside Tokyo DisneySea; Tokyo Disneyland Hotel and Disney Ambassador Hotel are an 8-minute walk from JR Maihama Station.

The six Disney Hotels

Disney Ambassador Hotel

The only Disney Hotel with a room for six. The Family Room is 97 m² with two sets of twin beds plus a trundle: five sleep at no extra charge and a sixth goes on an extra bed for a fee, and the bathroom has a jetted tub. The Junior Family Room (56 m², 4) is the step down. Children’s slippers, toothbrush, mug and step stool are in the room; children’s pajamas and a diaper bag are on request; a crib for infants under 18 months, a bed-wetting pad, a toddler toilet seat, a baby bath and a hot water thermometer are all available by reservation. It is an eight-minute walk from JR Maihama Station. official Family Room page

Tokyo Disneyland Hotel

Opposite the park entrance and eight minutes on foot from JR Maihama Station. The Family Room (Park View) is 93 m² for 5; the Junior Family Room is 57 m² for 4 with a triple bed layout; Disney’s Cinderella Room is 71 m² for 4 if the theming matters more than the floor space. Corner Rooms (Park View) are 59 m² and Turret Rooms 55 m², both for 4. official rooms page

Tokyo DisneySea Hotel MiraCosta

The hotel is inside Tokyo DisneySea — take the Disney Resort Line to Tokyo DisneySea Station, the third stop. For families the ceiling is four: the Harbor Rooms (Piazza View and Harbor View) are 60 m² for 4 with a “Hollywood twin” layout, two beds pushed side by side, which is the practical bit with a small child. No room type on the official list takes five. official rooms page

Tokyo DisneySea Fantasy Springs Hotel

Two wings on the Disney Resort Line at Bayside Station, the second stop. The Grand Chateau has the biggest rooms at the resort by floor area — Grand Room and Grand Terrace Room at 100 m² — but every Grand Chateau type takes only 4, as do the 70 m² Alcove, Terrace and Terrace & Alcove Rooms. The Fantasy Chateau is the one to look at with three children: the Deluxe Alcove Room (Partial View) is 50 m² for 5. Deluxe Rooms are 48 m² for 4 and Superior Rooms 41 m² for 4. Grand Chateau rooms · Fantasy Chateau rooms

Tokyo Disney Resort Toy Story Hotel and Tokyo Disney Celebration Hotel

Toy Story Hotel is at Bayside Station and is the compact option: Superior Rooms are 35 m² for 4 and every room type stops at four. Tokyo Disney Celebration Hotel is the value one, about 20 minutes from the parks on a complimentary shuttle; its “Discover” Quintet Room is only 29 m² but sleeps 5 on two regular beds, a daybed and a bunk bed, and the “Wish” Superior Room is 38 m² for 4. The six Official Hotels — inside the resort, shuttle to the monorail

Grand Nikko Tokyo Bay Maihama

The free Disney Resort Cruiser runs between the hotel and Bayside Station. On the Nikko Floor (11th–12th), the Nikko Bunk Bed Room is 64 m² for up to 6 and is the one with three or four children — you take your shoes off in it. The Nikko Grand Family Room is 85 m² for 4–5, and the Nikko Deluxe Family Room 56–64 m² for 4 with four beds. Family rooms also exist on the Rainbow and Garden floors. See rates · official site

Sheraton Grande Tokyo Bay Hotel

Over a thousand rooms, all of them over 36 m², and a room-type list built around bed counts rather than sizes — Standard, Park Wing, Ocean Dream, Grande and Sheraton Club all come in 2, 3 and 4 bed versions. For families the two to look at are Treasures 4–6 Beds (two doubles plus two bunk beds, or two twin) and Family Premium 5 Beds; the Japanese Suite sleeps 6 on two doubles or four twin futon. The hotel states that a child aged 6 or under may share one bed with one adult at no charge, that children aged seven and over are counted as adults, and that cribs are free with an advance reservation. Individual room sizes are not published. See rates · official site

Hilton Tokyo Bay and Hotel Okura Tokyo Bay

Hilton’s Happy Magic series is the family range: the Family Happy Magic Room Ocean and the Happy Magic Suite take up to 6, Family Happy Magic Park up to 5, and the hotel describes flexible bed layouts with an extra bed available. Room sizes are not stated on its official room list. Hotel Okura Tokyo Bay takes the opposite approach — every room is at least 44 m² and the sizes are published, but nothing sleeps more than four: Superior Rooms are 44 m² (up to 4 in the Type B and Type C layouts), the Japanese-style Room 44 m², the Junior Suite 53–56 m², the Senior Suite 62 m² and the Executive Suite 71 m². See rates · Hilton official site · See rates · Okura official site

MAIHAMA VIEW HOTEL and Tokyo Bay Maihama Hotel First Resort

MAIHAMA VIEW HOTEL (formerly Tokyo Bay Maihama Hotel) sells the only named connecting room type at the resort: the Connecting Harmony Room, 64 m² for 4–6, two rooms joined by an internal door, which the hotel recommends for three-generation trips. Symphony Rooms (35 m², 2–4) face the park side and Harmony Rooms (32 m², 2–3) the bay. Every room is a Hollywood twin — the two main beds are pushed together — and the hotel will put the main bed against the wall on request. Tokyo Bay Maihama Hotel First Resort runs free shuttles in three directions (Tokyo Disneyland, Maihama Station and Bayside Station) and its Deluxe Family Room is 50 m² for 4–6, with a Comfort Family Room at 49.8 m² for 4–6; its room pages are in Japanese only. The four Partner Hotels — Shin-Urayasu, about 15 minutes by free shuttle

Hotel Emion Tokyo Bay

The widest family range of any hotel in this guide. Family6 is 67 m² for 6 (four beds plus a hide bed and a sofa bed); the Japanese Twin Room [B Type] is 60 m² for 6 with two beds, a hide bed and three futon; the [A Type] is 57 m² for 5. Below those, Casual5 (56 m², 5), Casual4 (54 m², 4), the Sky Terrace Room (56 m², 4) and the Square Fourth rooms (38–42 m², 4) all take a family of four or five. See rates · official site

Urayasu Brighton Hotel Tokyo Bay

The free shuttle leaves from the 1F main entrance, needs no reservation and takes about 17 minutes. The At Home Room is 52 m² for 6 with a twin, a bunk bed and a day bed — the cheapest way in this guide to put six people in one room with a bunk. The Family Room is 48 m² for 4–5 (twin, twin and a sofa bed) and Room Forest ~Mix Style~ 42 m² for 5. See rates · official site

Mitsui Garden Hotel Prana Tokyo Bay and Oriental Hotel Tokyo Bay

Prana’s Executive, 5 beds, Terrace room is 60.8 m² for 5 and its Deluxe, 4 beds, Balcony room 36.4 m² for 4 — four proper single beds rather than a sofa bed, which matters with older children. Its shuttle leaves from stop #6 at the Tokyo Disneyland Bus Terminal East and takes about 20 minutes. Oriental Hotel Tokyo Bay runs up to 69 shuttle services a day, first-come first-served with no booking; its Marin Deluxe takes up to 4, and it states that children up to 6 (preschool age) may share a bed with paying adults, maximum two per room, with age 7 and over charged as adults. It does not publish room sizes. See rates · Prana official site · See rates · Oriental official site

Beyond these sixteen there are nine Tokyo Disney Resort Good Neighbor Hotels in Shinjuku, Koto/Sumida, Daiba/Ariake and Chiba/Makuhari. Their complimentary park shuttle requires a reservation, so they suit a trip where Disney is one day out of a week rather than the centre of it — Keio Plaza Hotel Tokyo, on our Tokyo family hotels list, is one of them. What actually decides this booking

  • Count beds, not the room size. Fantasy Springs’ Grand Rooms are 100 m² and take four; Celebration Hotel’s Quintet Room is 29 m² and takes five. Floor area tells you how comfortable the evening is, not how many people can legally sleep there.
  • Above four people, the choice narrows fast. Only one Disney Hotel room type takes six. If you are a family of five or six in one room, you are choosing among the Official and Partner hotels — or booking two rooms.
  • “Child” usually means six and under. Sheraton Grande and Oriental Hotel Tokyo Bay both draw the line at six years old for sharing a bed at no charge and count sevens as adults. Check the hotel you are booking; the Disney Hotels state occupancy per room type instead.
  • The shuttle is not the same everywhere. Official Hotels run a bus to a Disney Resort Line station, so you finish the trip on the monorail. Partner Hotels run a direct bus of about 15–20 minutes to the parks. Good Neighbor Hotels need a shuttle reservation.
  • Cribs are free but limited. Both the Disney Hotels and the Official Hotels that publish a policy take crib reservations in advance and cap the number; ask when you book, not on arrival.

Frequently asked questions

Which Tokyo Disney Resort hotel has the biggest family room?

Disney Ambassador Hotel's Family Room is the largest room for a family among the Disney Hotels: 97 m² for 6 people on two sets of twins plus a trundle, with five sleeping at no extra charge and a sixth on an extra bed for a fee. Tokyo Disneyland Hotel's Family Room (Park View) is 93 m² for 5, and Fantasy Springs Hotel's Grand Chateau has 100 m² Grand Rooms, but those top out at 4 people. Among the Official Hotels, Grand Nikko's Nikko Grand Family Room is 85 m² for 4–5 and its Bunk Bed Room 64 m² for up to 6.

Which hotels at Tokyo Disney Resort sleep six?

Disney Ambassador Hotel (Family Room, 97 m²), Grand Nikko Tokyo Bay Maihama (Nikko Bunk Bed Room, 64 m²), Hilton Tokyo Bay (Family Happy Magic Room Ocean and Happy Magic Suite), Sheraton Grande Tokyo Bay Hotel (Treasures 4–6 Beds and Japanese Suite 6 beds), MAIHAMA VIEW HOTEL (Connecting Harmony Room, 64 m²), Tokyo Bay Maihama Hotel First Resort (Deluxe Family Room, 50 m²), Hotel Emion Tokyo Bay (Family6, 67 m², and Japanese Twin Room B Type, 60 m²) and Urayasu Brighton Hotel Tokyo Bay (At Home Room, 52 m²).

What is the difference between a Disney Hotel, an Official Hotel and a Partner Hotel?

Tokyo Disney Resort groups its affiliated hotels by the benefits they give guests. The Disney Hotels are the six run by the resort itself and booked on its own site. The six Tokyo Disney Resort Official Hotels sit inside the resort and each runs a shuttle bus to a Disney Resort Line monorail station. The four Tokyo Disney Resort Partner Hotels are in the nearby Shin-Urayasu area with a complimentary shuttle of about 15 minutes to the parks. There are also nine Good Neighbor Hotels across Shinjuku, Koto/Sumida, Daiba/Ariake and Chiba/Makuhari whose complimentary park shuttle requires a reservation.

Can children share a bed at Tokyo Disney Resort hotels?

It depends on the hotel and you should check the one you are booking. Sheraton Grande Tokyo Bay Hotel states that a child aged 6 or under may share one bed with one adult at no extra charge, and that children aged seven or older are counted as adults. Oriental Hotel Tokyo Bay states that children up to 6 (preschool age) may share a bed with paying adults, a maximum of two children per room, and charges age 7 and over at adult rates. MAIHAMA VIEW HOTEL makes every room a Hollywood twin — the two main beds pushed together — specifically so a child can sleep alongside a parent.

Are cribs available at the Tokyo Disney Resort hotels?

At Disney Ambassador Hotel a crib for infants under 18 months is available by reservation, along with a bed-wetting pad, toddler toilet seat, baby bath and hot water thermometer; children's slippers, toothbrush, mug and step stool are in the room, and children's pajamas and a diaper bag are on request. Sheraton Grande Tokyo Bay Hotel provides cribs free of charge with an advance reservation and lends kids' yukata, step stools, baby bathtubs, a children's toilet seat and bedwetting sheets. Numbers are limited everywhere, so reserve when you book.

Is it worth staying at a Disney Hotel rather than an Official Hotel?

For a family the trade-off is room size against benefits. The Disney Hotels put you closest — MiraCosta is inside Tokyo DisneySea, Tokyo Disneyland Hotel and Disney Ambassador Hotel are an eight-minute walk from JR Maihama Station — but apart from the Ambassador's Family Room and Fantasy Springs' Fantasy Chateau, almost every Disney Hotel room type stops at four people. If you are five or six in one room, the Official and Partner hotels have more and larger options: Emion's Family6 (67 m²), Grand Nikko's Bunk Bed Room (64 m²), MAIHAMA VIEW's Connecting Harmony Room (64 m²) and Brighton's At Home Room (52 m²).
