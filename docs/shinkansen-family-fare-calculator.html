The rules this uses (2026)

  • Children 6–11 pay about half the adult fare (each part is halved and rounded down to the nearest ¥10; Green-car and sleeper tickets are full price). The exact amount is shown at purchase.
  • Children under 6 ride free — up to two per fare-paying adult — when they don't occupy a reserved seat. Their own reserved seat, or a third infant, needs a child ticket. Source: JR-Central. Confirm on the official site.
  • Big suitcases: on the Tokaido / Sanyo / Kyushu Shinkansen, baggage of 160–250 cm total (length + width + height) needs a free advance reservation for an "oversized baggage" seat. Over 250 cm isn't allowed. Reserve it when you book your seats.

JR rounds the basic fare and the limited-express charge down to ¥10 separately, so a real child ticket can differ from this estimate by a few tens of yen. Fares change and vary by route, season and seat type — this is an estimate based on the adult fare you enter. Always confirm the exact price on an official channel before you buy.
