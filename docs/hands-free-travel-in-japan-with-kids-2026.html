What is Luggage Forwarding?

Luggage forwarding, or takuhaibin, is a service that lets you send your bags ahead to your next stop, such as a hotel or the airport. This is particularly beneficial for families traveling with kids, as it lightens your load and reduces the stress of carrying everything. For example, prices for a standard suitcase typically start around ¥1,500 (about $15), but make sure to check the specifics based on your travel itinerary.

How to Use Luggage Forwarding with Kids

Using luggage forwarding is quite simple. First, locate a nearby service provider—these are usually found at major train stations like Tokyo Station or at airports like Narita and Kansai. You’ll need to fill out a form, pay the fee, and attach a label to your luggage. Ensure you double-check the drop-off times, as most services require that you send your bags out at least one day in advance for next-day delivery. This setup means you can keep your hands free for your little ones, which is especially helpful when navigating busy areas!

Cost and Booking Tips

As noted, prices start at around ¥1,500 for a standard suitcase. If you’re bringing a stroller, be aware that some services may charge extra, so it's a good idea to check beforehand. Booking is usually straightforward; you can reserve your service online or at your hotel. Be sure to allow a minimum of one day in advance for processing to avoid any last-minute issues.

Kid-Friendly Activities Near Drop-Off Locations

Once your luggage is on its way, you’re free to enjoy some fantastic family activities! For instance, if you’re dropping off your bags at Tokyo Station, you can head over to Tokyo Dome City, which features an amusement park and attractions that cater to younger children. In Kyoto, after sending your bags, take a leisurely stroll through the Arashiyama Bamboo Grove, where your kids can marvel at the towering bamboo and you can capture beautiful family photos!
