Hotel Ryumeikan Tokyo offers connecting rooms that include a 37.8㎡ Corner Deluxe Twin and a 32.5㎡ Standard Twin, accommodating up to 5 guests with an extra stacking bed. Reservations must be made directly by phone at (03-3271-0971) or email at reservation_hrt@ryumeikan.co.jp. The hotel provides amenities such as humidifying air purifiers and Nespresso machines, and preschool children can stay for free when sharing a bed with parents. For families with specific needs, it's recommended to inquire about the availability of specific room types before booking.

Imperial Hotel Tokyo features large family connecting rooms that span 143㎡, designed for 3 to 8 guests. Amenities include free high-speed internet, a mini-bar, and a Nespresso coffee machine to help you recharge after a day of adventures. Be sure to check for any seasonal promotions that might be available.

Karaksa Hotel TOKYO STATION offers unique Japanese Family Rooms with tatami mats and futon bedding, perfect for multi-generational families. Located just a 5-minute walk from Tokyo Station, it emphasizes convenience for families traveling with lots of luggage, allowing you to easily access various attractions without the hassle of long commutes.

Reservation Steps

For hotels like Hotel Ryumeikan Tokyo, remember that connecting rooms are limited. Always make your reservations directly to ensure availability and feel free to ask any specific questions about your needs, especially regarding room configurations or special requests.

Allergy Policies & Safety Information

Japanese hotels are generally accommodating regarding food allergies but do not guarantee allergen-free meals due to shared kitchens. Always inform the hotel of severe allergies well in advance, and for specific concerns, consider consulting your doctor or official guidelines before traveling. Some hotels allow guests to bring their own commercially packaged foods, which can be warmed. A Food Allergy Pre-Confirmation Form may be necessary for particular allergen concerns.

FAQ

1. What are the best family-friendly hotels in Tokyo?

Top options include Hotel Ryumeikan Tokyo, Imperial Hotel Tokyo, and MIMARU Tokyo Properties, all offering connecting rooms or kitchenettes.

2. Are there hotels with kitchenettes in Tokyo?

Yes, MIMARU Tokyo Properties and Condominium Hotel Keyakian Tokyo Nihonbashi provide kitchen facilities for families.

3. What amenities do family-friendly hotels offer?

Look for amenities like nursing rooms, diaper changing facilities, and kids' play areas.

4. How do I reserve connecting rooms in Tokyo?

Many hotels require direct contact for connecting room reservations, so call or email to confirm availability.
