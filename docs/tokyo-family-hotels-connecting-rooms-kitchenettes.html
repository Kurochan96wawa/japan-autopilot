Best Family-Friendly Hotels in Tokyo (2026)

If you're looking for the best family-friendly hotels in Tokyo, you can't go wrong with options that offer connecting rooms and kitchenettes. These amenities make it easier for families to relax and enjoy their stay. Here’s a curated list of hotels that cater specifically to families traveling with children.

  • Family-friendly hotels with kitchens and connecting rooms
  • Spacious accommodations for families of various sizes
  • Practical amenities for kids, including strollers and nursing facilities
  • Unique themed rooms for a fun experience
  • Options across popular Tokyo neighborhoods

Best Family-Friendly Hotels with Kitchenettes/Apartment-Style Accommodation

MIMARU Tokyo (various locations including Ueno, Ikebukuro, Kinshicho, Asakusa, Akasaka, Ginza East) is a top choice for families. All rooms are 40sqm or larger and include a fully equipped kitchen with cooking utensils, a microwave, and a large refrigerator. Some locations also provide coin laundry or in-room washing machines. For guests with allergies, MIMARU Tokyo Ueno NORTH is particularly recommended for its allergen-zero meal preparation capabilities. Prices for a Connecting Japanese Apartment (78 sqm) at MIMARU Tokyo Ueno EAST start around 80,000 yen per room for 2 adults (as of 2026, confirm on the official site).

Keyakian Tokyo Nihonbashi provides suite-style rooms that combine a living area with a traditional Japanese tatami room, perfect for little ones learning to crawl and explore. Each suite comes with a full kitchen featuring gas burners and ample space for family gatherings.

KOKO HOTEL Residence Asakusa Kappabashi is ideal for longer stays, featuring rooms that include kitchens and washing machines, with rates starting from 19,737 yen per night (as of 2026, confirm on the official site).

Hotels with Connecting Rooms

If you prefer more space while keeping the family close, consider hotels that offer connecting rooms. MIMARU Tokyo properties also feature these options, allowing families to enjoy separate sleeping areas while staying within reach. At Karaksa Hotel Tokyo Station, you’ll find connecting rooms for four, measuring 42.6 sqm, with free co-sleeping for children under 7. Starting prices are approximately 15,960 yen per person for 4 adults (as of 2026, confirm on the official site).
