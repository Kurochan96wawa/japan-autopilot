Visiting the Ghibli Museum and Ghibli Park with kids in Japan means two distinct, magical experiences that require careful advance planning for tickets and timed entry. A little logistical homework now will save you stress and delight your enthusiastic little ones!

TL;DR for Busy Parents

  • Ghibli Museum: Book tickets via Lawson Ticket on the 10th of the month, one month in advance, for a specific date and time. Strollers are not permitted inside exhibition areas, so a baby carrier is highly recommended. The famous Cat Bus Room is exclusively for elementary-aged children.
  • Ghibli Park: Secure tickets two months ahead via Boo-Woo Ticket, on the 10th of the month. Only Ghibli's Grand Warehouse has a timed entry; other areas may require queuing. Located within a larger public park, it offers more open space for strollers compared to the museum.
  • Both Locations: Confirm opening hours and closed days on official sites (as of 2026). Re-entry is not permitted at either venue once you've left.

Your family adventure into the enchanting worlds of Studio Ghibli in Japan will take you to two primary destinations: the whimsical Ghibli Museum in Mitaka, Tokyo, and the expansive Ghibli Park in Aichi Prefecture. Both celebrate the beloved animation studio, but they offer very different experiences for families.

Ghibli Museum (三鷹の森ジブリ美術館) with Kids

The Ghibli Museum offers a deeply immersive, art-focused experience—less like a theme park and more like stepping into a Ghibli film. Its intimate nature means strict visitor control, so securing tickets is your first quest.

Tickets & Reservations

Tickets for the Ghibli Museum operate on a reservation-only, date- and time-specific entry system. This means no spontaneous visits are possible, and you can't buy tickets at the museum itself. They are sold exclusively through Lawson Ticket, either via their website or at Loppi machines found in Lawson convenience stores across Japan. Plan to be ready on the 10th of each month at 10:00 AM (Japan Standard Time) when tickets for the following month go on sale. You'll need to enter within one hour of your designated entry time, but once you're inside, you can linger until closing. Be aware that changes or cancellations after purchase are generally not possible.

Ticket Prices

The pricing structure is straightforward, making it easy to budget for the whole family (as of 2026, confirm on the official site):

  • Adults/University students: ¥1,000
  • Junior high/High school students: ¥700
  • Elementary school students: ¥400
  • Infants (4 years and older): ¥100
  • Children under 4 years old are free.

There are no group or advance purchase discounts available.

Opening Hours & Closed Days

The museum is typically open from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Designated entry times are staggered throughout the day: 10:00 AM, 11:00 AM, 12:00 PM, 1:00 PM, 2:00 PM, 3:00 PM, and 4:00 PM. The museum is generally closed every Tuesday, and also observes longer closures for maintenance or holidays. Always check the official calendar well in advance of your visit.

Facilities for Families with Little Ones

  • Nursing Rooms (授乳室): Available for parents with infants. Please ask a staff member upon arrival for assistance.
  • Diaper Changing Facilities (おむつかえ台): You'll find these in multi-purpose toilets on each floor, as well as in the outdoor multi-purpose toilet and the cafe's multi-purpose toilet.
  • Stroller Access: Strollers are not allowed inside the exhibition areas of the museum. They must be left at a designated parking area near the entrance. If you're traveling with a baby or toddler, bringing a baby carrier is highly recommended – it's a game-changer for navigating the cozy interiors. Stroller rentals are not available on site.
  • Coin Lockers (コインロッカー): Located on the basement first floor (B1F), with 80 small and 16 medium coin-return lockers (they require a ¥100 coin, which you get back after use). For larger luggage, consult a staff member for assistance.
  • Child-Specific Areas: The famous "Cat Bus Room" is a dream come true for younger fans, but it has a specific rule: it's exclusively for elementary school children (6th grade and under). Parents cannot enter, but staff are on hand to supervise children during their exhilarating 5-minute play session.
  • Allergy Information: Specific allergy labeling rules are not detailed on the official Japanese website for the Straw Hat Cafe, which offers "warm home cooking." If your child has severe allergies, it's wise to carry familiar snacks and consult with staff directly about ingredients upon arrival.
  • Photography: Photography and videography are strictly prohibited inside the museum to encourage a fully immersive experience. You are welcome to take photos in the outdoor areas.
  • Re-entry: Once you leave the Ghibli Museum, re-entry is not permitted.
  • Parking: There is no dedicated parking at the museum. Public transportation is highly encouraged. A convenient community bus runs frequently from Mitaka Station South Exit.

Navigating Tokyo with a stroller or carrier is much smoother when you know these specifics. And remember, sometimes a well-timed snack can be as magical as a Ghibli film when warding off a toddler tantrum!

Ghibli Park (ジブリパーク) with Kids

Unlike the intimate Ghibli Museum, Ghibli Park sprawls within the vast Aichi Earth Expo Memorial Park (Morikoro Park) in Aichi Prefecture. This much larger, spread-out experience features multiple themed areas, focusing less on traditional museum exhibits and more on exploring landscapes and buildings inspired by Ghibli films.

Tickets & Reservations

Like the museum, Ghibli Park operates on a reservation-only ticket system, and tickets are not sold on-site. You'll need to purchase tickets in advance via the online ticket sales site "Boo-Woo Ticket" or at Loppi machines in Lawson and Ministop convenience stores nationwide. For the popular "Ghibli Park Large Walk Ticket," tickets go on sale from 2 months prior, on the 10th of the month at 2:00 PM (Japan Standard Time). Only the "Ghibli's Grand Warehouse" area requires a specific timed entry, where you must enter within one hour of your designated time with all members of your party. Other areas do not have specific entry times but may require queuing during busy periods. All entries to the park must be completed by 4:30 PM. Generally, changes or cancellations after purchase are not possible, and if the ticket applicant cannot visit, the ticket will be invalid.

Ticket Prices

The "Ghibli Park Large Walk Ticket" covers all five areas of the park:

  • Adults: ¥3,500 (weekdays), ¥4,000 (weekends/holidays)
  • Children (4 years old to elementary school students): ¥1,750 (weekdays), ¥2,000 (weekends/holidays)
  • Children under 3 years old are free.

Opening Hours & Closed Days

Ghibli Park's operating hours vary slightly:

  • Weekdays: 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM
  • Weekends, Holidays, and Weekdays during School Long Holidays: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM

The park is closed every Tuesday (or the next weekday if Tuesday is a public holiday), and also observes closures during the New Year period. Always consult the official calendar for specific dates.

Family-Friendly Aspects of Ghibli Park

Being part of a large public park, Ghibli Park offers a different dynamic for families. The expansive outdoor areas, themed zones like Youth Hill, Dondoko Forest, Mononoke Village, and the Valley of Witches, provide more space for kids to roam and explore. While specific stroller rules for *inside* every building within Ghibli Park may vary, the overall environment of Morikoro Park is very stroller-friendly, with wide pathways. You'll find ample restrooms and more varied food options across the larger park area. Frequently Asked Questions for Ghibli with Kids

Can I buy tickets for the Ghibli Museum or Ghibli Park on the day of my visit?

No, both the Ghibli Museum and Ghibli Park operate on a reservation-only, advance ticket system. Tickets are not sold at the venues, so plan to purchase them well in advance online or via convenience store machines in Japan.

Is the Ghibli Museum stroller friendly for toddlers?

Strollers are not permitted inside the Ghibli Museum's exhibition areas. There is a designated stroller parking area at the entrance. For young children, a baby carrier is highly recommended for navigating the museum's interior.

What is the Cat Bus room, and can parents join their kids?

The Cat Bus Room at the Ghibli Museum is a special play area exclusively for elementary school children (6th grade and under). Parents cannot enter, but staff supervise the children during their 5-minute play session.

How far in advance should I book tickets for Ghibli Park?

Tickets for Ghibli Park, especially the "Ghibli Park Large Walk Ticket," go on sale two months prior to the desired visit date, on the 10th of the month at 2:00 PM JST. Booking as soon as they become available is advisable due to high demand.

Are there nursing rooms available at the Ghibli Museum?

Yes, the Ghibli Museum provides nursing rooms for parents. Please inquire with staff upon arrival for assistance and directions.

Can I leave and re-enter Ghibli Museum or Ghibli Park?

No, re-entry is not permitted at either the Ghibli Museum or Ghibli Park once you have exited the premises.

What's the best way to get to the Ghibli Museum with kids?

Public transportation is recommended as there is no dedicated parking. From Mitaka Station South Exit, a convenient community bus runs directly to the museum.

A well-prepared parent is a happy parent! Always confirm all details on the official websites before your magical Ghibli journey.
