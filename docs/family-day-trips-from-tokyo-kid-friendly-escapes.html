If you’re looking for easy and enjoyable day trips from Tokyo with children, Moominvalley Park, Saitama Children’s Zoo, and Grinpa are excellent choices. These destinations offer a delightful mix of fun, adventure, and a taste of Japanese culture, all easily accessible by train from the city!

  • Moominvalley Park - A magical experience for fans of the beloved Moomin characters.
  • Saitama Children’s Zoo - A spacious zoo designed for child-friendly interactions.
  • Grinpa - An amusement park near Mt. Fuji with attractions for young children.
  • All locations provide stroller-friendly options.
  • Convenient public transport access makes these trips easy to manage.

Moominvalley Park (ムーミンバレーパーク) – Hanno, Saitama Prefecture

Immerse your family in the enchanting world of Moomin with a visit to Moominvalley Park. Located in Hanno, Saitama, this theme park offers a whimsical experience for children and adults alike.

Ticket PriceDetails
Adults (junior high school students and above)1,500 yen (tax included)
Children (4 years old to elementary school age)1,000 yen (tax included)
Children aged 3 and underFree

Stroller Access & Rental: Strollers are permitted inside the park; however, some indoor facilities with stairs may not be accessible. Stroller rental is available at the Information Center for those who need it.

Nursing Rooms: Available at several locations within the park, including a beautifully decorated room at the Moominvalley Park Information Center, equipped with changing tables and other amenities.

Estimated Visit Duration: Expect to spend around 2-3 hours exploring the park, but if you wish to enjoy the attractions and the atmosphere fully, you might want to stay longer.

Access Note: The park is part of a larger area known as Metsä Village, and it requires about a 15-minute walk from the parking area to the park entrance.

Saitama Children’s Zoo (埼玉県こども動物自然公園) – Higashimatsuyama, Saitama Prefecture

This spacious zoo is designed with children in mind, offering opportunities for interaction with animals and extensive play areas. It’s a fantastic destination for a day of family fun!

Admission PriceDetails
Adults (high school students and above)500 yen
Children (elementary and junior high school students)200 yen
PreschoolersFree

Opening Hours: The zoo's hours vary by season; please check the official website for the latest schedule. Note that the zoo may close on Mondays and Tuesdays in January and February.

Nursing Rooms: There are three nursing room locations equipped with diaper changing facilities and hot water dispensers for formula preparation.

Stroller Access & Rental: Strollers are allowed throughout the park and can be rented for 500 yen per day, although availability is limited.

Internal Transport: The Ayapo Road Train operates within the park for easy movement; the fare is 200 yen for ages 3 and older.

Grinpa (ぐりんぱ) – Susono, Shizuoka Prefecture (Near Mt. Fuji)

Situated at the base of Mt. Fuji, Grinpa is an amusement park that features attractions and activities suitable for families with young children, including themes like Sylvanian Families and Ultraman.

Admission PriceDetails
Admission OnlyAdults 1,100 yen, Children 750 yen
One-Day CouponAdults 3,300 yen, Children 2,450 yen

Nursing Rooms: Available at three locations within the park, equipped with changing tables and hot water on request.

Stroller Access & Rental: Strollers are allowed inside the park, and rentals are available for those who need them.

Dietary Accommodations: The park’s restaurants offer children’s menus; for allergy information, please inquire directly with the staff.

FAQ

What are the best day trips from Tokyo for kids?

Moominvalley Park, Saitama Children’s Zoo, and Grinpa are excellent options for family-friendly day trips from Tokyo.

Are these destinations stroller-friendly?

Yes, each location offers stroller access and rental options.

How do I get to these places from Tokyo?

All locations are accessible by public transport, typically via train, making travel straightforward.

What amenities are available for young children?

Each destination has nursing rooms, diaper changing facilities, and kid-friendly dining options.
