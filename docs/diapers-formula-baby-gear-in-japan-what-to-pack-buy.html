If you're traveling in Japan with young children, you'll be happy to know that diapers, formula, and baby gear are readily available. You can pack light by purchasing these essentials locally. After all, who wants to carry a heavy suitcase filled with baby items through the airport? Picture navigating the bustling Tokyo subway while your toddler is asking for snacks!

What to Pack

As you prepare for your trip, focus on bringing only the essentials. Here’s a practical list:

  • Diapers: Bring enough for your journey, but remember that you can find diapers in most convenience stores and supermarkets across Japan. Brands like Pampers and Moony are widely available and trusted by parents.
  • Formula: If your child relies on formula, pack enough for the travel days. Once in Japan, stores like Akachan Honpo and Ito Yokado carry popular brands such as Meiji and Snow Brand, which meet international standards. Prices typically range from ¥1,500 to ¥3,000 for a can, but check for current prices.
  • Snacks: Pack familiar snacks to keep your little ones satisfied during long train rides or city explorations. While Japanese convenience stores offer a variety of tasty snacks, having some of their favorites on hand can make a difference.

What to Buy in Japan

Once you land in Japan, buying baby supplies is both convenient and budget-friendly. Here are some recommended places to shop:

  • Akachan Honpo: This dedicated baby goods store provides a vast selection of diapers, wipes, formula, and baby gear. With over 100 locations nationwide, you can easily find one in major cities like Tokyo and Osaka. As of 2026, a pack of 80 diapers costs around ¥4,000, but be sure to confirm prices on their official website.
  • Ito Yokado: A well-known supermarket chain with a robust baby section. They carry everything from baby food to clothing and toys, often at competitive prices. Look out for promotions on baby products.
  • Convenience Stores: Don't overlook the convenience of 7-Eleven, FamilyMart, and Lawson! These stores stock diapers, baby wipes, and even baby food, which can be a lifesaver when you need something in a hurry.

Renting Baby Gear

If you prefer to travel light, consider renting baby gear. This option allows you to have a stroller or car seat waiting for you, eliminating the hassle of transporting it from home. Services like rent a stroller, car seat & baby gear in Japan make it easy to reserve equipment online ahead of time, ensuring you have everything you need upon arrival.

Safety Tips

Keeping your young children safe while traveling is crucial. Here are some important safety tips:

  • Always use a car seat when traveling by car. While taxis in Japan usually have seat belts, bringing your own car seat is the safest option.
  • Be mindful of local customs regarding children in public spaces. Generally, Japan is family-friendly, and many public restrooms are equipped with changing tables.
  • Before trying new foods, check for any allergies or dietary restrictions your child may have. Japanese baby food is often labeled in Japanese, so using a translation app can help you understand the ingredients.

FAQ

Are diapers available in Japan?

Yes, diapers are widely available in convenience stores, supermarkets, and specialty baby shops.

What types of baby formula can I find in Japan?

Popular brands like Meiji and Snow Brand meet international standards and are readily available.

Can I rent baby gear in Japan?

Yes, baby gear rental services allow you to rent strollers and car seats easily.
