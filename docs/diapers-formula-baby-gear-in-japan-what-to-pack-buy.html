When traveling to Japan with young children, you can find a wide selection of diapers, formula, and baby gear, which makes it easy to travel light. You won't have to worry about running out of supplies, as you can buy what you need locally. Excitingly, some vending machines even dispense baby formula!

  • Pack enough diapers and formula for travel days.
  • Consider buying baby gear locally for convenience.
  • Popular stores include Akachan Honpo and Nishimatsuya.
  • Rent strollers and car seats easily via local services.
  • Don’t forget snacks—your toddler’s best bargaining tool!

What to Pack

Before you head off on your adventure, make sure to pack the right essentials. Here’s a quick checklist for families with toddlers and infants:

ItemsQuantity
Diapers20-30 (for travel days)
FormulaEnough for 3-5 days
Baby wipes1-2 packs
SnacksVariety for travel

You might want to bring a few diapers and some formula for the journey, but you can easily find additional supplies once you arrive. Major stores like Akachan Honpo (赤ちゃん本舗) offer a wide selection of baby products, from diapers to baby food, and even maternity items. There are over 160 Akachan Honpo locations throughout Japan, making it easy to find a store near you (as of 2026, confirm on the official site).

What to Buy Locally

In Japan, you’ll find numerous baby products readily available, often at reasonable prices. Here are some local options worth considering:

  • Diapers: Brands like Moony and Goo.N are popular, offering excellent absorbency and comfort. Expect to pay around ¥3,000 for a pack of 40 diapers (as of 2026, confirm on the official site).
  • Formula: Look for brands such as Meiji and Morinaga, which are commonly found in supermarkets and convenience stores. A 700g can typically costs about ¥2,500.
  • Baby Gear: Stores like Nishimatsuya (西松屋), known for affordable baby clothing and gear, offer a vast selection. Expect to find items like strollers starting at approximately ¥10,000.

Renting Baby Gear

If you prefer not to lug all your baby gear across the globe, renting is a convenient option. You can easily rent a stroller, car seat & baby gear in Japan through services like Klook, allowing you to book online ahead of your trip. This way, you can have everything waiting for you at the airport or your accommodation.

Snacks and Meals

Japan is a goldmine of snack options. For toddlers and young children, familiar snacks can be a lifesaver. Look for:

  • Pocky: These chocolate-covered biscuit sticks are a hit with kids.
  • Onigiri: Rice balls wrapped in seaweed can easily be found at convenience stores.
  • Fruits: Apples and bananas are widely available and make for nutritious snacks.

Be mindful of local dining etiquette—many restaurants have child-friendly menus and high chairs available, but it's wise to check ahead, especially in smaller establishments.

FAQ

Can I find baby formula in convenience stores?

Yes, major convenience stores like 7-Eleven and FamilyMart carry baby formula, including popular brands.

Are diapers readily available in Japan?

Absolutely! You’ll find a wide range of diapers at supermarkets and baby specialty stores.

What baby gear can I rent in Japan?

You can rent strollers, car seats, baby carriers, and even cribs through various rental services.

Are there baby-friendly restaurants in Japan?

Yes, many restaurants are accommodating to families and offer high chairs and child-friendly menus.

Can I use my own baby wipes in Japan?

Yes, bringing your own wipes is convenient, but you can also purchase baby wipes locally.

Is it safe to use public transportation with a stroller?

Yes, Japan's public transportation is stroller-friendly, with elevators and designated areas for families.

Where can I buy baby gear in Japan?

Stores like Akachan Honpo and Nishimatsuya are excellent for baby gear, clothing, and supplies.

What should I do in case of an emergency?

Locate the nearest hospital or clinic upon arrival, and keep emergency contact numbers handy.

Traveling with children in Japan can be a delightful experience, especially with the right preparations. Keep your essentials light, enjoy the local offerings, and embrace the adventure!
