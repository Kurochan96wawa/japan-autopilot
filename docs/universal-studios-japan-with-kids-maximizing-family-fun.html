Universal Studios Japan is a fantastic destination for families, offering a mix of thrilling rides, interactive attractions, and beloved characters that will delight kids of all ages. Getting There

Reaching Universal Studios Japan is straightforward. The park is conveniently located in Osaka, just a 10-minute train ride from Osaka Station to Universal City Station. The JR Yumesaki Line is the way to go, and it’s stroller-friendly—no need to worry about those pesky stairs with a toddler in tow.

Stroller Rentals and Accessibility

If you’re traveling with little ones, consider renting a stroller to make your day smoother. Strollers can be rented at the park for around ¥1,000 (as of 2026, confirm on the official site), which can save you from the hassle of bringing one along. The park is largely accessible, with ramps and elevators available, making it easy to navigate with a stroller or wheelchair.

Dining Options

Dining can be a challenge with picky eaters, but Universal Studios Japan has plenty of options. You’ll find everything from Japanese cuisine to Western favorites. For a quick bite, try the Mel’s Drive-In for burgers and fries, or head to the park’s many stalls for street food like takoyaki (octopus balls) and yakitori (grilled chicken skewers). Don’t forget to check out the themed desserts—who can resist a Minion cupcake?

Safety and Logistics

Safety is a priority at Universal Studios Japan. The park regularly cleans and maintains its attractions, and there are plenty of staff on hand to assist. If your child gets separated from you, the park has a lost child area located at the Guest Services center. Make sure to equip your child with a wristband or a card with your contact information—just in case.

Maximizing Your Visit

To make the most of your time, consider purchasing express passes to skip the lines for popular attractions. These can be booked in advance online or at the park. Arriving early is also a great strategy; the park opens at 8:30 AM, so getting there close to opening time can help you hit the most popular rides before the crowds arrive.

FAQ

What is the age range for rides at Universal Studios Japan?

Rides vary in age recommendations, with attractions like Minion Park suitable for all ages, while others may require children to be at least 6 or 8 years old.

Are there stroller rentals available?

Yes, strollers can be rented at the park for approximately ¥1,000.

What dining options are available for kids?

There are numerous dining options, including burgers, Japanese street food, and themed desserts to cater to various tastes.

How do we handle safety if we get separated?

The park has a lost child area at Guest Services, and it's advisable to equip children with a wristband or card with your contact information.

Can we purchase express passes in advance?

Yes, express passes can be purchased online or at the park to skip lines for popular attractions.

What are the park hours?

The park typically opens at 8:30 AM, but hours can vary, so check the official website for the latest information.

Is Universal Studios Japan stroller-friendly?

Yes, the park is mostly accessible with ramps and elevators for easy navigation with strollers and wheelchairs.

What’s the best time to visit for fewer crowds?

Visiting on weekdays, especially during school sessions, tends to be less crowded than weekends or holidays.
