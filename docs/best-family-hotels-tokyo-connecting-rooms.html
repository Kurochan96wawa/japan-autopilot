Connecting Rooms

Connecting rooms are ideal for families who need extra space. Typically, these rooms feature two separate rooms with a door between them, allowing parents to enjoy some privacy while keeping an eye on their kids. Prices generally range from 25,000 to 40,000 yen per night (as of 2026, confirm on the official site). Many hotels provide cribs and stroller access, making it easier for families traveling with toddlers or young children. Just be aware that this option can be pricier compared to other accommodations, so plan your budget accordingly.

Apartment Hotels

If your little ones are picky eaters, an apartment hotel with a kitchenette can be a lifesaver. Prices for these accommodations usually fall between 15,000 and 30,000 yen per night. With a kitchenette, you can prepare meals that cater to your children's tastes, and many of these hotels also feature laundry facilities. While you may forgo some hotel amenities, the extra space and flexibility of an apartment hotel often make it a worthwhile investment for families.

Hotels Near Tokyo Disney Resort

For families planning a visit to Tokyo Disneyland or DisneySea, staying near the resort can greatly enhance your experience. Prices for these hotels typically range from 20,000 to 35,000 yen per night. Many hotels in this area offer shuttle services to the parks, themed experiences for kids, and even special kids’ menus. However, keep in mind that this area can be quite crowded, especially during peak seasons, which may lead to longer travel times if you decide to venture into the city.

JR/Metro Hub Hotels

Hotels located near major JR or metro hubs are a fantastic choice for families eager to maximize their sightseeing experiences. Prices at these hotels generally fall between 18,000 and 30,000 yen per night. The major advantage of these hotels is their proximity to public transportation, which makes navigating the city with kids and strollers much simpler. However, be prepared for potentially busier and noisier environments, especially during rush hour.

FAQ

1. Are connecting rooms worth the extra cost?

Absolutely! Connecting rooms provide more space and privacy, which can be a relief for parents traveling with kids.

2. Can I find kid-friendly food options nearby?

Most hotels offer kids’ menus, and apartment hotels allow you to cook meals that cater to picky eaters.

3. Is it easy to get around Tokyo with a stroller?

Yes, many hotels are stroller-friendly, and public transportation usually accommodates strollers well, though it's always wise to check specific station accessibility.

4. Do hotels near Disney offer shuttle services?

Yes, many hotels provide complimentary shuttle services to Tokyo Disneyland and DisneySea, making it convenient for families.

5. What’s the average size of hotel rooms in Tokyo?

Rooms can be smaller than Western standards, but connecting rooms or apartment hotels can help alleviate space concerns.

6. Is breakfast included in these hotels?

Some hotels include breakfast, but it's best to check each hotel's amenities before booking to avoid surprises.
