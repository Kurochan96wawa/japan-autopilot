FAQ

1. How much data do I need for my family in Japan?

For a family of four, around 10-15 GB should be sufficient for navigation, translation, and occasional streaming. Always consider your family's specific usage patterns.

2. Can I share the eSIM with my kids' devices?

Yes, most eSIM plans allow hotspot/tethering, which means you can share data between devices easily.

3. How easy is it to set up an eSIM?

Setup is usually simple, with QR codes provided for scanning. Klook and Airalo have particularly user-friendly processes that parents will appreciate.

4. Are eSIMs available for short trips?

Yes, many eSIMs offer plans valid for a week or longer, making them perfect for short visits to Japan.

5. What happens if I run out of data?

You can typically top up your plan directly through the provider’s app or website, which makes it convenient to manage your usage.

6. Can I use my home phone number with an eSIM?

Yes, eSIMs can operate alongside your home number, ensuring you won't miss important calls or messages while abroad.

7. Is it worth getting an eSIM instead of a local SIM card?

Yes, eSIMs offer more flexibility, easier setup, and generally better coverage for travelers, making them a great choice for families.

In conclusion, for families visiting Japan, the Klook eSIM stands out as the best option, balancing price, data needs, and ease of use.
