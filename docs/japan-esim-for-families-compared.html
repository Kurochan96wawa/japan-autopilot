How to Purchase an eSIM

Purchasing an eSIM is straightforward. You can buy it online before your trip or at the airport upon arrival. If you choose to purchase at the airport, look for kiosks or counters specifically offering eSIM services. Both Narita and Haneda airports have these facilities available. Setting up your eSIM usually involves scanning a QR code sent to your email, which takes just a few minutes—ideal for that early morning arrival with a sleepy toddler!

Staying Connected with Kids

Staying connected on the go can be a lifesaver when traveling with kids. Use your eSIM to access maps, find the nearest parks, or keep the little ones entertained with their favorite shows during long train rides. FAQ

What is an eSIM?

An eSIM is a digital SIM that allows you to activate a cellular plan without needing a physical SIM card.

Can I use my existing phone in Japan?

Most modern smartphones support eSIM technology, but be sure to confirm compatibility with your carrier before your trip.

Is data coverage reliable in Japan?

Yes, both Softbank and DOCOMO provide extensive coverage across urban and rural areas, though it's advisable to check the latest coverage maps for your specific routes.

How do I activate my eSIM?

Activation usually involves scanning a QR code you receive via email, which takes just a few minutes to complete.

Are there age restrictions for eSIM purchase?

No, anyone can purchase an eSIM, but a valid ID may be required for activation, especially for minors.

Can I switch between eSIMs while in Japan?

Yes, you can switch eSIM profiles on your device as needed, depending on your data plan and usage.

What if my kids' devices don’t support eSIM?

Consider a portable Wi-Fi device as an alternative to keep everyone connected throughout your travels.

Is it easy to find Wi-Fi in Japan?

Yes, many cafes and public places offer free Wi-Fi, but having your own eSIM is more reliable and convenient.

Staying connected during your family adventure in Japan can make a world of difference. With the right eSIM, you can navigate easily, keep the kids entertained, and make the most of your trip!
